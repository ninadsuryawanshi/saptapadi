# Saptapadi Design System

## Color Palette
Based on the traditional yet premium aesthetic of the Saptapadi brand:

- **Crimson (#8B1A2C)**: Primary brand color. Used for headings, primary buttons, and accents. Represents tradition and passion.
- **Gold (#C9963A)**: Secondary accent. Used for borders, icons, and subtle highlights. Represents luxury and sacredness.
- **Cream (#FBF6EE)**: Primary background color. Softer than pure white, providing a warm, premium feel.
- **Deep (#1A0A0F)**: Text and dark background color. A rich, dark brown/black for high contrast.
- **Muted (#7A5C40)**: For secondary text and descriptions.

## Typography
- **Heading (Serif)**: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Cinzel`.
  - Use `Cinzel` for logos and strictly decorative headings.
  - Use `Cormorant Garamond` for main page headings and emotive text.
- **Body (Sans-Serif)**: `DM Sans`.
  - Used for readability in forms, details, and general body text.

## UI Components
- **Glassmorphism**: Use `backdrop-filter: blur()` for navigation bars and floating panels.
- **Borders**: Thin, 1px borders using low-opacity Gold or Crimson.
- **Gradients**: Subtle linear gradients between Crimson and Deep for sections like the Hero.

## Animations
- **Framer Motion**:
  - Fade-up animations for section entry.
  - Smooth transitions between registration steps.
  - Micro-interactions on button hovers.
